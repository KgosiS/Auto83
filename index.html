Practical Z83 application guides

Use these guides to prepare your information before opening a vacancy application. Requirements differ between adverts, so treat the advert and the official form as the final source of instructions.

Before you apply

Turn a vacancy advert into an application checklist

Read the entire advert before filling in a form. Note the exact job title, department, reference number, closing date and delivery method. A department may advertise several similar jobs with different reference numbers, so copy the reference for the specific post you want.

Make a short checklist of required attachments, certification rules, subject lines, postal or email addresses, and any instruction about one application per post. Keep a copy of the advert until you have submitted the application.

Profile preparation

Information to gather for your reusable profile

Collect your identity and contact details, education history, employment dates, professional registration details where relevant, and contact information for three referees. Use consistent spelling and dates across your profile and supporting documents.

Ask referees for permission before sharing their contact information. Confirm that their telephone numbers and email addresses are current. Do not list someone as a referee without checking that they are willing and prepared to respond.

Qualifications

Record education clearly and accurately

For each qualification, record the institution, qualification name and year in a way that matches the supporting certificate. If a qualification is incomplete or still in progress, label it honestly rather than presenting it as completed.

Keep certificates and transcripts available in the format requested by the advert. Only submit certified copies when instructed, and check the advert for certification age limits or other document rules.

Employment history

Make dates and job descriptions easy to verify

Write down each employer, position title and period of employment before completing the Z83. Use month and year where the form or advert asks for them. If you have a gap, be ready to describe it truthfully if the application asks.

Keep job titles consistent with reference letters or contracts. Do not inflate responsibilities or claim experience you cannot substantiate. The generated document is a draft and may still need fields completed by hand.

Vacancy details

Why role, department and reference details matter

These details connect your form to the correct vacancy. Enter them exactly as shown in the advert, including meaningful punctuation and reference-number prefixes. Avoid replacing a reference number with a general department number or an unrelated advert code.

Auto83 asks you to enter these details for each application because they change from vacancy to vacancy. Review them on the generated form before you send it.

Final review

A careful check before sending your application

  1. Compare the job title, department and reference number with the advert.
  2. Check your name, ID, contact details, dates, answers and referee details.
  3. Complete any blank sections that apply to you and sign/date the form where required.
  4. Attach every document requested and use the exact submission method in the advert.
  5. Keep a copy of the final form and proof of submission.

Frequently asked questions

Understand what Auto83 does—and what you should still check yourself.

Is Auto83 a government recruitment website?

No. Auto83 is an independent form-preparation tool and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the South African Government or a recruiting department. We do not advertise vacancies, select candidates or decide application outcomes.

Does generating a Z83 mean my application is complete?

Not necessarily. The generated PDF is a draft populated with information Auto83 supports. Review the entire form, add missing answers, sign it where required, and attach documents according to the vacancy advert before you submit.

Does Auto83 submit applications for me?

No. You must submit the completed form and supporting documents to the office or channel specified in the official advert. Check that the advert is still open and keep your own proof of submission.

Can I use the same profile for every vacancy?

Your personal and career history can be reused, but the post title, department and reference number are vacancy-specific. Enter and verify these for each application, then review the generated form for accuracy.

What should I do if the generated form has missing or incorrect information?

Correct your saved profile and generate the form again, or complete the PDF manually if the field is not supported. Never submit information you have not checked against your records.

How is my account information handled?

Auto83 uses Firebase Authentication and Firestore for account and profile features, and browser storage for profile data used by the app. Review our Privacy Notice before entering personal information.
