Privacy Notice

Last updated: 27 October 2026

This notice explains how the Auto83 website handles information when you browse the public pages or use profile and Z83 preparation features. Auto83 is an independent application-preparation tool, not a South African government service.

Before entering sensitive information: an Auto83 profile can include an identity number, contact details, employment history, referee details and a signature image. Only enter information you are comfortable storing through this site. Access to cloud profile data depends on the Firebase project configuration and its security rules.

Information you provide

When you create an account or complete a profile, the site may process account email, name, South African identity number, phone number, education and employment records, application-specific vacancy details, referee contact details, and a drawn signature. Passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication and are not written into the Auto83 Firestore profile document by the site.

Where information is stored

  • The site uses browser storage to keep a local working copy of the profile and a short-lived cache of public news headlines. Browser storage remains on the device until cleared by the site or browser.
  • When signed in, profile information is saved in the Auto83 Firebase project's Firestore database. Firebase Authentication manages sign-in. These are Google Firebase services subject to their own infrastructure and terms.
  • The news section requests public South African article metadata from FreeNewsApi through Auto83's Firebase Function. The request contains a country filter, not your profile or application information. Headlines link to external publishers whose privacy practices are separate.

How information is used

Profile information is used to display and save your profile and to prepare a Z83 PDF at your request. Vacancy circulars are not uploaded by the current manual-entry flow. The news feed is independent of your account profile. Auto83's current website code does not include Google AdSense advertising or analytics scripts.

Third-party services and external links

The website loads libraries and fonts from third-party content delivery networks, uses Firebase for account and profile features and the news proxy, and the proxy contacts FreeNewsApi for headlines. Those services may receive technical request information such as an IP address and browser details. When you follow an external link, the publisher's privacy notice applies.

If advertising is added later, this notice must be updated before launch to explain the advertising providers, cookies or similar technologies, available controls, and any consent choices required in the regions where the site is used. Google explains its advertising technologies in its Advertising and privacy information.

Keeping your information accurate and managing it

You can edit profile fields in the profile page and clear site data from your browser settings. Clearing browser data does not by itself delete the Firestore profile associated with an account. The current website does not provide an in-app account or cloud-profile deletion control. Do not post personal details in public support channels.

Security and retention

Auto83 uses Firebase services, but no internet transmission or storage method can be promised completely secure. Cloud access is controlled by the Firebase project's security rules; those rules must be correctly configured and maintained by the site operator. Do not use the generated PDF without checking its contents, and keep your own copies of important application records.

Information may remain in browser storage or the cloud profile until it is changed or deleted through the relevant service or by the project operator. The site currently does not publish a fixed deletion schedule.

Children and intended use

Auto83 is intended for adults preparing job applications. It is not designed to collect information from children.

Changes and contact

This notice may change when the website's features or service providers change. For questions about the project, use the Auto83 project issue tracker. Do not include an identity number, password, signature or other private information in a public issue.
